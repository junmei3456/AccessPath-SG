AccessPath SG

An AI-Assisted Planning-Support System for Senior Pedestrian Accessibility in High-Density Public Housing
Tiong Bahru–Havelock, Singapore
Read Final Report (PDF) ↗ Planning Dashboard ↗ Navigation App ↗

Abstract

Singapore’s ageing population highlights the importance of accessible pedestrian journeys in mature public housing estates. However, conventional pedestrian network data may not capture physical barriers and street-level conditions that affect everyday mobility.

Focusing on the Tiong Bahru–Havelock area, this project explores how artificial intelligence can complement Geographic Information Systems (GIS) through the analysis of Street View Imagery. AccessPath SG combines AI-generated observations with geospatial information in two prototypes: a Planning Dashboard that supports the review of potential accessibility issues, and an Elderly Navigation App that presents pedestrian route options. The project demonstrates the potential of AI-assisted accessibility assessment while acknowledging limitations in imagery coverage, feature identification and route verification. Human review remains essential before the results can inform real-world planning decisions.
